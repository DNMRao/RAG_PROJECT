# Refunds And Duplicate Payments

Northstar refunds duplicate payments when two captured transactions correspond
to the same invoice. The requester must provide the invoice number and both
payment references within 30 calendar days of the second charge.

Finance reviews a complete duplicate-payment request within three business
days. Approved refunds return through the original payment method; bank
processing may take a further five to ten business days.

A dissatisfaction with course content is not automatically a billing error.
Requests unrelated to duplicate payment or an incorrect invoice must be
escalated to the programme operations team for review under the enrolment
terms.
