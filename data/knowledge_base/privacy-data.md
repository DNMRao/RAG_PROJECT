# Handling Personal Information

Support may collect the minimum information needed to resolve a request, such
as a registered email address or invoice number. Never include passwords,
authentication tokens, backup codes, passport scans, or medical details in a
language-model prompt.

Before using a support message in an approved AI workflow, redact names, email
addresses, telephone numbers, payment references, and other direct
identifiers. Report suspected disclosure of personal information through the
security incident channel.
