# Replacing A Multi-Factor Authentication Device

When a phone is lost, replaced, or reset, support can reset multi-factor
authentication only after identity verification. The requester should open a
support ticket from the registered email address and describe the lost-device
situation.

Support never asks for an existing backup code, password, passport scan, or
identity document in chat. After verification, the previous authenticator is
disabled and the requester enrolls a new device at the next sign-in.
